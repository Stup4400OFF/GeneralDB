## Recommended Protein Intake Amount
_For each of your objectives_
| **Objective**                  | **Math**    | **Example** |
|--------------------------------|-------------|-------------|
| Recovering (Operation/Surgery) | 1.5g per kg | 70kg = 105g |
| Recommended                    | 0.9g per kg | 70kg = 63g  |
| Muscle Building                | 1.7g per kg | 70kg = 119g |
